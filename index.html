About us

Rooted started in 2019 on a rooftop in Kondapur with about thirty pots and a lot of trial and error. Today we run a nursery on the city's west side and look after gardens for homes, cafés and small offices.

We only stock plants that do well in Hyderabad's heat and short monsoon. If something won't last in your space, we'll say so instead of selling it to you.

  • 6 years growing in Hyderabad
  • 400+ gardens and balconies cared for
  • 150 plant varieties in stock

Services

Pick one, or mix them. Most customers start with a few plants and add garden care later.

Plants and pots

Indoor, outdoor and low-light plants, repotted for you and ready to place. Free care card with every purchase.

Delivery within Hyderabad is free above ₹999. Every plant has a 14-day health guarantee.

Balcony makeovers

We visit, measure the light, and set up a layout that suits your space and budget.

Site visit is free. Most balconies take one day to set up, including pots, soil and drip watering.

Monthly garden care

A gardener visits twice a month to prune, feed, treat pests and refresh soil.

Plans start at ₹1,200 a month. Pause or cancel any time when you travel.
